How to Download Your Response Sheet?

  1. 1

    Log in to MHT-CET Portal

    Go to the official CET Cell website (cetcell.mahacet.org) and sign in using your registration details.

  2. 2

    Open Objection Tracker

    Navigate to "Objection Tracker" or "Candidate Response Sheet" link for MHT-CET 2026 and click to view your response sheet.

  3. 3

    Download & Save the File

    On Desktop (Chrome/Edge): Press Ctrl + S (Windows) or Cmd + S (Mac). Choose "Webpage, Single File (*.mht)" or "HTML Only" and save it.

    On Android (Chrome): Tap the 3-dot menu at the top-right and select the Download icon () to save it in your Downloads folder as an .html/.mhtml file.

    On iOS (Safari): Tap the Share icon → tap Options (near the top) → choose Web Archive → tap Done → select Save to Files.
